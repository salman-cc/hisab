Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026
Applies to Hisab 0.2.2-dev and its current development-preview features.

Your local ledger stays on your phone unless you choose to export it or copy it to a connected Google ledger. AiCoN does not run a server that receives your ledger.

Hisab is a development preview. Google sign-in, Drive round trips and Android background behavior have not completed real-device testing. Google access availability depends on the OAuth project's testing and publication settings. This policy describes the implemented design, not a guarantee of uninterrupted sync or data recovery.

1. Information you record

Your ledger can contain amounts, dates, categories, accounts, notes, names of people, loans, repayments, budgets, goals and customization settings. Manual entry does not require a bank connection. English category suggestions run locally from rules and matching notes; your notes are not sent to an AI service.

2. Storage on your phone

Hisab stores working ledgers and settings in the app's private Android storage. The local-only ledger is separate from cached Google ledgers. Android's automatic app-data backup is disabled. Uninstalling removes the app's local data, but does not delete files you exported, or necessarily delete data already stored in Google Drive. Protect your phone and keep independent backups.

3. Optional Google account access

If you connect Google, the current implementation requests openid, userinfo.email and userinfo.profile, plus drive.appdata to manage only Hisab's own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive. It verifies the account subject, verified email and email address from Google's user-info response; it does not read or use the Google profile name or photo. The requested userinfo.profile scope appears unused by the current code and is retained pending an owner-reviewed OAuth change. Hisab does not request access to all your Drive files. Google handles the account sign-in and consent; Hisab does not ask for or receive your Google password.

The verified account subject is used to keep ledgers separate, and its email is shown in the account screens on this device. Any optional profile name you set inside Hisab is a separate, device-only app setting. Access tokens are held in app memory for Google API calls and are not sent to an AiCoN server. Reconnection may be required.

4. What is sent to Google Drive

When you enable and use a Google ledger, its entries, budgets, goals, custom items, preferences and sync metadata travel directly between your phone and Google's APIs over HTTPS. Copying the separate local-only ledger requires an explicit choice. Merely connecting Google does not upload it.

Hisab's app-data files are hidden from the ordinary Drive file browser. They are not the same as an encrypted export, and Hisab does not promise application-level end-to-end encryption for Drive sync. Google stores and processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. There is no AiCoN intermediary ledger server.

Google user data is used only to provide account selection, authentication and the optional sync features you choose. Hisab does not sell it, use it for ads, or transfer it to an advertising or analytics service. Hisab's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Photos, contacts and reminders

Profile photos and an optional display name are saved on this phone. If you choose to link a person to a contact, Android may request Contacts permission. Hisab reads only the contact you select, not your whole address book. Contact links and phone numbers, profile photos, private display names and saved pay-by notes stay outside Drive sync and encrypted ledger backups.

Optional local notifications ask Android's notification permission. They show generic reminder text rather than financial amounts or names. Reminder delivery can be delayed by Android. SMS and WhatsApp reminders show the recipient and words for review, then open the selected messaging app. You must send them there; your messaging provider's terms and fees apply. Version 0.2.2-dev has no SMS permission or automatic message sender. Hisab does not read SMS, notifications or screen contents.

6. Exports and encrypted backups

CSV and PDF exports are unencrypted and may contain names, debts, notes and other financial information. Anyone with a copy can read them. PDF is a printable image statement; CSV and PDF cannot restore customization.

Passphrase-encrypted backups use AES-GCM with a PBKDF2-derived key. They include ledger records, custom items and supported customization settings, but not device-private profile/contact/pay-by information or Android permissions. AiCoN cannot recover your passphrase. You choose the export destination through Android's file picker. A cloud provider or app you choose to save or share with processes that file under its own policy.

7. Sync, deletion and disconnecting

Sync can wait for connectivity, battery conditions, Android scheduling, renewed Google authorization or your review of conflicts. A local deletion is not a completed remote deletion until the relevant sync or reset succeeds.

  • Delete entries in the selected ledger to remove their financial content. Small content-free record IDs and sync/deletion markers can remain to stop old offline copies from restoring deleted data.
  • Use the app's Google-ledger reset action to remove that account's ledger contents. Content-free reset/deletion markers remain for the same protection. Other devices must reconnect to learn of the reset. Your separate local-only ledger and exported files are unaffected.
  • Disconnect Google access to stop sync and request revocation of Hisab's permissions for that account. Disconnecting does not itself erase your existing Drive ledger or separate offline cache. Reset the cloud ledger first if you want to delete its contents.
  • You can also remove Hisab's access through your Google Account's third-party connections controls. Google Drive's app management can remove hidden app data where available. Revoking access alone is not the same as deleting every copy.
  • Deleting app data does not delete backups, CSV/PDF files, messages or other copies you previously saved or shared. Manage those separately with their providers.

Google may retain information according to its own retention rules. Hisab cannot promise erasure from Google's internal backups.

8. No ads or analytics

The Hisab app contains no ads, advertising identifiers, analytics service, referral tracking or sale of ledger data. This informational website uses no cookies, analytics scripts, forms or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, logs visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub's own privacy practices. Clicking external links or contacting support uses those services.

9. Support and policy updates

Hisab is developed under the AiCoN name. Privacy and support contact: aicon4tech@gmail.com. If you email support, the information you choose to send is handled to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, backup passphrases or unnecessary financial records.

Material changes to data practices will be explained in an updated policy and in the app where needed. New access or sharing is not authorized by this policy alone.
